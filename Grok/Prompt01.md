**Cloud Support** refers to the technical assistance provided for cloud-based services and applications to ensure they operate smoothly, efficiently, and securely.

It covers help with Infrastructure as a Service (IaaS), Platform as a Service (PaaS), and Software as a Service (SaaS). Support teams typically assist with troubleshooting issues, performance optimization, security concerns, integration problems, usage guidance, billing questions, and operational best practices.

### Key Aspects
- **Provided by cloud service providers** (such as AWS, Microsoft Azure, and Google Cloud) or third-party specialists/Managed Service Providers.
- **Common offerings** include documentation, community forums, and paid support plans with different response times and service levels. For example:
  - Google Cloud’s Customer Care ranges from free Basic Support (documentation, community, and billing help) to paid Standard, Enhanced, and Premium plans that add 1:1 technical support, faster response times for priority issues, and extra services like third-party technology support.
- **Cloud Support Engineers/Associates** act as the primary contact for customers. They monitor systems, resolve technical problems, maintain performance and security, and help users get the most from cloud resources.

### Cloud Support vs. Managed Cloud Services
Cloud Support is generally more reactive—focused on resolving incidents, answering questions, and providing guidance related to the cloud platform itself. Managed Cloud Services go further: they often involve ongoing management, monitoring, optimization, and strategic partnership so the customer’s internal team does not handle day-to-day cloud operations.

In short, Cloud Support helps organizations use and maintain cloud technologies effectively, minimize downtime, and maximize the value of their cloud investments.
