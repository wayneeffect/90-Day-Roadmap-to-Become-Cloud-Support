**In the United States, Cloud Support roles (such as Cloud Support Engineer, Associate, or Specialist) typically pay a median or average total compensation of roughly $100,000–$140,000 per year, with significant variation based on experience, company, location, and specific title.**

### Cloud Support Engineer (most common technical role)
Recent data (primarily 2025–2026) shows:
- **Glassdoor**: Median total pay around $135,000–$138,000 (typical range $115,000–$169,000), including base + additional pay (bonuses, stock, etc.).
- **Salary.com** (as of August 2026): Average base-focused salary of about $124,673 (majority range $115,500–$133,600; top earners ~$142,000).
- **Talent.com**: Average around $105,000 (entry-level starting near $74,000; experienced up to ~$164,000).

**At major providers** (total compensation often higher due to bonuses and equity):
- Amazon/AWS Cloud Support Engineer: Median total pay frequently reported in the $174,000–$176,000 range (base often $110,000–$125,000 + significant additional pay).
- Microsoft: Median total around $159,000.

**By experience level** (approximate U.S. ranges):
- Entry-level (0–2 years): $80,000–$115,000
- Mid-level (2–5 years): $115,000–$150,000
- Senior/Lead (5+ years): $150,000–$200,000+

### Cloud Support Associate (often more entry-level, e.g., AWS)
- Median total pay commonly $97,000–$123,000.
- Amazon/AWS-specific figures often show base around $70,000–$85,000 with total compensation near $97,000–$126,000 depending on the exact data set.

### Cloud Support Specialist
- Generally lower or more variable: Glassdoor median total around $102,000–$103,000; some aggregated sources report averages closer to $50,000–$90,000 for broader or less technical specialist roles.

### Key Factors Affecting Pay
- **Location**: Higher in high-cost areas (e.g., San Francisco/San Jose, Seattle, New York) — often 20–30%+ above national averages.
- **Company**: Big Tech/cloud providers (AWS, Microsoft, Google, Snowflake) typically offer higher total packages with stock/RSUs.
- **Certifications & skills**: AWS, Azure, or GCP certifications, plus scripting (Python/Bash), networking, and security knowledge, can boost pay.
- **Additional compensation**: Bonuses, equity, and benefits often add $20,000–$50,000+ at larger firms.

**Outside the U.S.**, salaries are generally lower. For example, in the UK, entry-level roles might range £35,000–£50,000, mid-level £50,000–£75,000, and senior £75,000–£100,000+.

Salaries fluctuate with market conditions, inflation, and demand. For the most accurate current figures for a specific role, location, or company, check sites like Glassdoor, Levels.fyi, Salary.com, or Indeed, and consider total compensation (not just base salary).
